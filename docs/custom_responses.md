# Grimjaw Visitor Prompt Catalog

A canonical reference guide of distinct spoken prompts and questions that visitors can say to interact with the Grimjaw animatronic skeleton.

---

## 1. Movies, TV & Pop Culture

### I'm Gonna Git You Sucka

- How much for one rib?
- I sure am hungry.
- I'm gonna git you sucka!

### Say Anything

- Hey my brother, can I borrow a copy of your Hey Soul Classics?

### Blade Runner

- Can you recite the Tears in Rain quote from Blade Runner?

### Scary & Horror Quotes

- Give me a scary quote.
- What big eyes you have!

### Movie & Pop Culture Favorites

- What's your favorite movie quote?
- What's your favorite movie?
- Who is your favorite horror movie character?
- Did you see yourself in Night of the Living Dead?
- How do you feel about sand?
- What's your favorite TV show?
- How would you like a nice greasy pork sandwich?
- You're stewed, butt-wad!
- How do you feel about He-Man and Skeletor?
- Give me an Ali G quote.
- What does Ali G say about skeletons?
- Do you have any questions for C. Everett Koop?
- Have you tried organizing a holiday swap with Jack Skellington?
- Who's your favorite superhero?

## 5. Skeleton Anatomy & Calcium

### Structure & Senses

- What happened to your hair?

- How can you hear without ears?
- How can you talk without vocal cords?
- How do you smell without a nose?

- Where is your stomach?

### Bones & Cold

- Do you get cold without any skin?
- Do you miss your skin?

- Can you get sunburned?
- Are there bones inside me too?
- Do your bones click when you move?
- Why are your teeth so white?
- How tall are you?
